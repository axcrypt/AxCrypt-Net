## Rules for forks and community builds

You are welcome to fork and build this software. If you distribute a modified
or independently built version, you must:

1. **Not claim it is an official AxCrypt build.** Only builds produced and
   digitally signed by AxCrypt AB are official.
2. **Rename your distribution** (for example "MyCrypt, based on AxCrypt
   source code") if there is any risk of confusion, and replace the AxCrypt
   logo and brand assets with your own.
3. **Not suggest endorsement** by AxCrypt AB, in the product, its metadata,
   or its marketing.
4. **Not sign builds** in a way that implies they originate from AxCrypt AB.

Truthful, nominative references such as "based on AxCrypt source code
(GPL-3.0-or-later)" are permitted and appreciated.
